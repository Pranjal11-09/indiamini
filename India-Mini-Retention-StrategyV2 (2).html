◀ BACKTHE CORE IDEA ▶
2Core · anticipation · the open loop

Kal Ka Clue

Every mini ends with one cell left adhoora — deliberately unfinished. Its clue points at tomorrow's theme. You leave with something unresolved, so you come back to close it.

Today's leftover clue:
"Friday's reel runs on this — 5 letters."
↻ Carries to tomorrow's mini
HypothesisAn open loop (one unresolved clue) lifts next-day return more than a streak — it runs on curiosity, not fear.
TestCarried-over-clue cohort vs a normal closed mini. Measure D1 return + whether they open to finish it.
MetricD1 / D7 return; % who resolve the leftover; churn after a missed day vs the streak group.
Cheap testNo new engine — manually theme one clue per day to tease the next. Ship to a small cohort first.
◀ BACKWHERE IT LEADS ▶
3Core · the payoff they chase

The Adhoora Mini

Each day's leftover cell is saved. Across a week, the seven of them assemble into their own bonus mini — "Hafte Ka Raaz." Miss a day? That cell's just blank, so the bonus is a little easier. No punishment, only payoff.

↓
Locked · solve the week to assemble
HypothesisA weekly payoff built from your own week gives a reason to return all 7 days — chased like a reward, not feared like a streak.
TestWeekly-assembly cohort vs daily-only. Watch return-rate climb as the week's grid fills.
MetricD7 retention; days-played per week; bonus-mini solve rate & the return spike it creates.
Cheap testFake-door it — show the assembling grid with a static bonus mini. If weekly return lifts, build real per-user assembly.
