3 IDEAS TO RETAIN INDIA MINI SOLVERS
1 — Solver DNA
Reward competence, not check-ins
Mastery · inspired by Chess ELO & Garmin readiness
Show solvers they're getting better — fastest category (films? food?), solve-time trend, “you crack Bollywood clues 40% faster than last month.” People return for proof of skill, not a number.
Test → competence dashboard vs none · measure D30 retention.
2 — Aaj ka adhoora
The completion loop
Completion + autonomy · inspired by Apple activity rings
A soft “4 / 7 themed minis this week” ring — an open loop they want to close, not a streak they fear breaking. Miss a morning, no punishment, so it never becomes a chore.
Test → completion ring vs classic streak · measure churn after first miss.
3 — Chhupa rustam clue
Anticipation over fear
Variable reward · why surprise beats loss-aversion
Occasionally one clue unlocks a surprise — a blink-and-miss desi pop-culture bonus or a harder mini. They know a treat might come, never when. Anticipation retains longer than the dread of a broken streak.
Test → variable bonus vs fixed · measure D7/D14 return & session frequency.
